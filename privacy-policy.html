Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what data Try AI GPT ("we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. This policy covers Try AI GPT and its tools, currently AGS DM Insta (Instagram comment-to-DM automation). The service is operated by [company name].

1. Data we collect

Account data: your email address, name (if you provide it) and sign-in method (email or Google).

Instagram data (only after you connect your account and approve access): your Instagram account ID, username and account type; comments on your posts and Reels (comment text, commenter username and ID); messages in your Instagram conversations; and basic post details such as caption, link and thumbnail.

Content you create: message templates, keyword rules, lead notes, tags and support tickets.

Usage and technical data: automation events, message delivery logs, plan usage counters and error logs.

Payment data: payments are handled by Easebuzz. We receive your payment status, amount and transaction ID, and your name, email and mobile number are shared with Easebuzz to process the payment. We do not receive your card, UPI or bank details.

2. How we use it

  • To run the service: detect keyword comments and send the private replies you configure, show your inbox, leads and analytics.
  • To manage your account, plan limits, billing and support requests.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix errors.

We do not sell your data or your followers' data. We do not use Instagram data for advertising or to train AI models.

3. Instagram and Meta

We access Instagram only through Meta's official APIs, only with permissions you grant, and only to provide the features you use. You can revoke access at any time in Instagram (Settings, Apps and websites) or by disconnecting the account inside Try AI GPT. Your use of Instagram is also governed by Meta's terms and policies.

4. Who we share data with

We use these service providers, who process data only on our behalf: Supabase (database, authentication, backend functions), Netlify (website hosting), Easebuzz (payments) and Meta (Instagram API). We may disclose data if required by law. We do not share your data with anyone else.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored in a managed database with row-level access rules so each workspace can see only its own data. Instagram access tokens are kept in a restricted backend table that is not accessible from the browser. We use HTTPS for all traffic. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. Disconnecting Instagram deletes the stored access token. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and workspace data (see Data Deletion), normally within 30 days. We may keep limited records where the law requires, for example payment records.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data. In the app you can edit your profile and download your workspace data from Settings. For anything else, or to withdraw consent, contact us at [contact email]. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the GDPR, and you may complain to your data protection authority.

8. Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage only to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.

9. Children

Try AI GPT is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data in other countries. We rely on their security commitments and contractual safeguards.
