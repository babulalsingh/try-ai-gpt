User Data Deletion

Effective date: October 16, 2026

You can disconnect Instagram and delete your data from Try AI GPT at any time. Here is how, and what happens.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app

  1. Sign in to Try AI GPT.
  2. Open Settings (on mobile: the Settings tab).
  3. In the Data card, select Request account deletion and confirm.

This sends us a deletion request from your verified account. Before deleting you can download your data with Download my workspace data (JSON).

Option 2: Email us

Send a request from the email address you signed up with to [contact email] with the subject "Delete my data". If you do not have an account but believe we hold data about you from Instagram, include your Instagram username so we can find it.

Option 3: Remove Instagram access only

To stop all access without deleting your account, in Try AI GPT open Connect Instagram and select Disconnect. You can also remove the app in Instagram under Settings, Apps and websites. When you disconnect, we delete the stored access token and pause the rules for that account.

What we delete

  • Your profile and sign-in account.
  • Your workspace: connected Instagram account details and tokens, templates, rules, leads and tags, conversations and messages, automation logs, and support tickets.

We complete deletion within 30 days of a verified request and confirm by email.

What we may keep

Payment and invoice records that we must keep to meet legal and tax obligations, and backups that are overwritten on their normal schedule. Payment details held by Easebuzz are governed by Easebuzz's own policy.
